This is a repository for the roblox game An Adventure at the Mall. The game is a roguelite adventure game about conquering scenarios at a mall. Test it out here: https://www.roblox.com/games/98899210633034/An-Adventure-at-the-Mall



How to run:
Go ahead and open the website. Once you are there, hit the big blue play button to launch the game.

If you don't want to create an account or verify your age (cause roblox requires that), go ahead and download a version of the game yourself. You can do this by hitting the three dots just to the right of the name and clicking "Edit in Studio." Note that you have to have Roblox studio installed. Once you open the project, open your asset manager (home -> assets) and click "places in experience." Double-click "The Mall (terra demo)" to open it. Then you can test by clicking the blue arrow in the upper left hand corner of the editor.
